Design proposal notes — not part of the page

  • Palette: one signature amber on warm near-black. Amber is rationed — it marks only today, the current value, and the primary action. Everything else is warm neutrals, which is what keeps it professional instead of loud.
  • Type: Space Grotesk for display numbers/headlines (bold, slightly techy — the youthful energy), Inter for body. Uppercase letter-spaced micro-labels carry the "performance hardware" feel without copying Whoop.
  • Engagement without noise: the readiness ring animates on load, the ticker strip moves, sleep bars have hover tooltips, cards lift on hover. All motion respects prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Amber contrast was validated at ≥ 3:1 against the dark surface; charts are single-hue with direct labels, so no legend is needed.
