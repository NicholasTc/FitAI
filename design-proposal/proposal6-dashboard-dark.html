Design proposal notes — not part of the page

  • Color has meaning, not decoration: ember = strain, action, and caution (Push Day, buttons, elevated RHR, short sleep); violet = recovery and depth (sleep stages, HRV, check-in, confidence). Neutrals carry all structure. A signal's color tells you which side of the push/recover ledger it sits on.
  • Depth without noise: black base with two fixed ambient glows (ember top-right, violet bottom-left), gradient hairline borders on the two interactive callouts (check-in, coach), and a single gradient moment — the readiness ring running ember → violet.
  • Charts: sleep stages are one hue at three opacity steps with gaps (not three colors); sparklines are 2px single-hue with a dashed baseline and one labeled endpoint; the 7-day bars direct-label only today. Ember/violet text steps validated ≥ 3:1 on the black surface.
  • Explainability: the hero now says which signals produced the score and that check-in is pending — the "High confidence" tag earns its meaning, and each signal card's footer connects back to today's recommendation.
  • Motion: one-time staggered card entrance, animated ring + count-up, pulse on sync. All honor prefers-reduced-motion.
